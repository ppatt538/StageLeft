The mobile UI is improved structurally, but I do not like the current theme. Please keep the layout improvements, but redesign the visual theme so the app feels calmer, cleaner, and more premium.

What feels wrong in the current theme:
- the background has a muddy brown/olive cast
- the dark cards feel too heavy
- the white top and bottom bars feel disconnected from the dark middle section
- the blue active tab feels too sharp compared to the rest of the palette
- overall it feels more like a dark media app than a calm AI desk

New direction:
Create a “warm paper desk” theme.

Theme goals:
- calm, premium, intelligent
- warm off-white / paper-like background
- subtle layered neutral surfaces
- dark or semi-dark cards that still feel elegant, not heavy
- a restrained accent color, preferably muted blue or slate blue
- no muddy brown, no olive cast, no neon accents
- cohesive header, content area, and bottom composer so the whole screen feels like one design system

Color direction:
- app background: warm paper / off-white
- section surfaces: soft cream / stone
- cards: deep graphite or warm charcoal
- borders: subtle soft gray
- text: strong contrast but not stark black everywhere
- accent: muted blue, slate, or subdued teal
- checklist/reminder/link/media colors should be more muted and refined

Specific styling changes:
- remove the muddy dark brown page background
- unify the header, content area, and composer into one coherent palette
- make cards feel lighter and more premium
- reduce harsh contrast transitions between sections
- make the active tab styling feel integrated with the theme
- refine icon background circles so they feel subtle and elegant, not bright
- preserve readability and hierarchy

Do not redesign the layout. Only change theme, color system, contrast handling, and visual styling so it feels like a premium calm productivity app.

1. the new theme direction
